# Road-Accident-Dashboard
📊 **Road Accident Dashboard — Key Insights & Interactive Summary**

I built an interactive data analytics dashboard to explore road accident patterns and casualty distribution across multiple dimensions — time, location, vehicle type, road conditions, and environment factors.

---

## 🚦 Overall Impact

* **Total Casualties:** 417,883
* **Slight Injuries:** 351,436 (84.1%)
* **Serious Injuries:** 59,312 (14.2%)
* **Fatalities:** 7,135 (1.7%)

👉 Insight: The vast majority of accidents are *non-fatal but high-volume*, meaning prevention policies should focus on frequency reduction rather than only severity reduction.

---

## 🚗 Vehicle Type Analysis

| Vehicle | Casualties |
| ------- | ---------- |
| Car     | 333,485    |
| Bicycle | 33,672     |
| Bus     | 12,798     |
| Tractor | 1,032      |
| Other   | 3,424      |

👉 Insight: Cars dominate accident involvement — targeted driver awareness & urban traffic management can dramatically reduce overall accidents.

---

## 🛣 Road & Surface Conditions

**Road Type Contribution**

* Single carriageway: 309.7K (Highest risk zone)
* Dual carriageway: 67.4K
* Roundabout: 26.8K
* One-way street: 7.4K
* Slip road: 4.7K

**Road Surface**

* Dry: 279,445
* Wet: 115,261
* Snow/Ice: Very low but high severity risk

👉 Insight:
Accidents occur mostly on normal dry roads — meaning behavior & traffic density matter more than weather alone.

---

## 🌆 Location & Light Condition

* Urban: 255.9K

* Rural: 162.0K

* Daylight: 305K

* Dark: 112.9K

👉 Insight:
Most accidents happen **in daylight & urban areas** — visibility isn't the main problem; traffic volume and human decision-making are.

---

## 📅 Monthly Trend (YoY)

* Consistent rise from January → October
* Peak around October–November
* Drop in December

👉 Insight: Seasonal travel & festive movement increase accident probability.

---

## 🧠 Key Takeaways

✔ Accidents are a frequency problem, not just a severity problem
✔ Urban traffic management > weather control
✔ Cars are the primary focus for prevention
✔ Daytime driving still risky due to congestion
✔ Preventive awareness campaigns should target peak months
